zktls notarize HTTP over MPC-TLS

Browser-side MPC-TLS notarization over WebTransport against a local ledger. Verifier runs on the host; the page emits a structured event stream as it works.

Stwo leaves folded pairwise in a binary PCD tree across a worker pool. Each step's proof embeds the verifier of its children. Pool size via ?pool=N.
